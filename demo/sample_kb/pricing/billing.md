# Billing & Invoices

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express for all plans. Business and Enterprise plans can also pay by ACH (US), SEPA (EU), or wire transfer (any region). Set up alternative payment at **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing period and emailed to every user with the **Billing admin** role. You can download past invoices as PDF from **Settings → Billing → History**. The last 36 months of invoices are retained; older invoices are available on request from support.

## Tax

We collect VAT for EU and UK customers, GST for Australian customers, and US sales tax in states where we have nexus. To add a tax ID to your invoices, go to **Settings → Billing → Tax** and enter your VAT/GST/EIN number. The tax ID is validated against the relevant government registry and may take up to 24 hours to appear on invoices.

## Refunds

Annual plans are refundable on a pro-rated basis within 30 days of the renewal date. After 30 days, the current period is non-refundable, but you can cancel to stop the next renewal. Monthly plans are not refundable — you can cancel to stop future charges.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, we retry on day 3, day 7, and day 14. After three failures the workspace enters a 7-day grace period — read-only for all users, no new tasks or comments. After grace, the workspace is suspended; data is retained for 90 days before deletion.
