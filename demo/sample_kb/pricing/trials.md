# Free Trials

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start.

## What's included in the trial

You get every feature of the plan you're trialling. There are no user caps and no project caps during the trial — if you want to evaluate Business with 50 people, that's the supported path.

## Trial extensions

You can request a one-time 14-day extension by emailing trials@cumulus.example from your workspace admin address. We grant extensions for evaluations involving more than 25 users or a security review. We do not grant a second extension; instead, please convert to a paid plan and we'll credit any unused days if you cancel within the first 14 days post-conversion.

## What happens when the trial ends

Three days before the trial ends, all admins receive a reminder email. If you add a payment method, the workspace converts automatically and the first invoice is generated on the conversion date. If you don't, the workspace drops to the Free plan: any projects beyond the Free limit of 3 become read-only, and history older than 14 days is hidden (not deleted — it returns if you upgrade later).

## Trial limitations

Some Enterprise-only features — CMEK, PrivateLink, audit log export — are not available in self-serve trials. Contact sales@cumulus.example to arrange an Enterprise proof-of-concept.
