# Notifications

Cumulus delivers notifications through three channels: in-app inbox, email, and Slack.

## What triggers a notification

By default you are notified when:

- You are mentioned in a comment.
- You are assigned a task or a task is reassigned away from you.
- A task you watch changes status.
- A sprint you participate in starts or ends.
- An automation explicitly notifies you.

You automatically watch any task you create, comment on, or are assigned to. You can stop watching from the task's right-hand panel.

## Quiet hours

Set quiet hours under **Settings → Notifications → Quiet hours**. During these hours, no email or Slack notifications are sent — they queue and deliver as a single digest at the end of the quiet window. In-app inbox notifications continue to arrive in real time.

## Email digest

If you'd rather not get per-event emails, switch to the daily digest under **Settings → Notifications → Email**. The digest delivers at 8 AM in your selected time zone and summarises everything from the prior 24 hours.

## Slack

Connect Slack under **Settings → Integrations → Slack**. Each user authorises individually. Notifications go to your Slack DMs by default; admins can set channel-level rules for project-wide events.

## Turning everything off

You can pause all notifications with the bell icon in the top bar. Pauses default to 1 hour but can be set up to 7 days. Mentions still appear in the in-app inbox but won't push.
