# Quickstart

Get from zero to your first task in Cumulus in under five minutes.

## Step 1: Create a workspace

Sign up at app.cumulus.example with your work email. Cumulus will create a workspace using the domain part of your email — `acme.com` becomes `acme.cumulus.example`. You can rename it later from **Settings → Workspace**.

## Step 2: Invite your team

From the sidebar, click **People → Invite**. Paste email addresses one per line. New members receive an invitation email with a link that expires after 7 days. If you need to resend, open the People page and click **Resend invite** on the row.

## Step 3: Create your first project

Click the **+** next to **Projects** in the sidebar. Give the project a name (e.g. "Mobile App v2"), pick a color, and choose a template. The **Sprint** template is the most common starting point.

## Step 4: Add tasks

Inside a project, press `c` to create a task. At minimum, fill in the title and assignee. Press `Cmd+Enter` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Enter` (Windows) to save.

## Step 5: Start a sprint

From the project page, click **Start sprint**, pick a date range, and drag tasks into the sprint backlog.

That's the loop. Everything else in Cumulus — automations, dashboards, integrations — builds on these four primitives.
